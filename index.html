Hyeongwoo Nam

Integrated M.S./Ph.D. Student, Mechanical Engineering
Machine Learning & Control Systems Lab, Yonsei University

I study how robots can remember, reason about, and act in the physical world with foundation models. I am interested in organizing a robot’s accumulated experience, such as observations, conversations, and notes, into a memory that LLM and VLM agents can search and reason over, and in turning such knowledge into reliable plans through structured world models and symbolic planning.
